Account Deletion

App Name: Credentials

We respect your privacy and provide users with the ability to request permanent deletion of their Credentials account and associated personal data.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Credentials account by sending an email to our support team.

Please include the following information:

  • Your registered account email address.
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request.
  • A clear request to permanently delete your account.

Our support team may contact you to verify your account ownership before processing your request.

Request Account Deletion

2. What Information Will Be Deleted?

After your deletion request is verified and processed, the following information associated with your account will be deleted:

  • Firebase Authentication account information.
  • Registered email address and profile information.
  • Saved credentials stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Other personal information associated with your account.

3. Data Retention

We will delete associated personal information and saved credentials as part of the account deletion process, except where retention is required by applicable laws or legitimate legal obligations.

4. Account Deletion Processing

Once we receive your request, we will verify your account ownership and process the deletion. We will notify you if additional information is required.

5. Can Deleted Accounts Be Recovered?

No. Once your account and associated data have been permanently deleted, they cannot be recovered. You may need to create a new account to use the application again.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. Please save or export any information you want to keep before submitting your deletion request.
