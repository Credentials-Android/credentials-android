Account Deletion Request

This page explains how users of Credential Manager can request deletion of their accounts and associated data.

Delete your account

You can delete your account directly from the Android app.

  1. Open Credential Manager.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Navigate to Settings or Account Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

Your Firebase Authentication account and associated Firestore credentials data will be deleted through the app's account deletion process.

What data is deleted?

  • Account information associated with your account.
  • Saved login credentials stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Other associated account data covered by the deletion process.
