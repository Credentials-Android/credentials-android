Account deletion request

This page explains how Credentials users can delete their accounts and request deletion of associated data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Credentials and sign in.
  2. Open the dashboard navigation menu.
  3. Select Delete Account and confirm.

This process deletes your Firebase Authentication account, profile, saved credentials, and activity logs associated with your account.

Request deletion if you cannot sign in

Email us from the email address associated with your Credentials account. Include that account email in your request, but do not include your password or saved credentials. We will verify the request before processing deletion.
