Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Credentials ("we", "our", or "the app") is a secure credentials management application that helps users store and manage their login information.

1. Information We Collect

We may process the following information:

  • Email address used for Firebase Authentication.
  • Account profile information, if provided.
  • Login credentials saved by users in the application.
  • Information necessary to operate and secure the app.

2. How We Store and Protect Information

User-saved credentials are encrypted in the application using AES-GCM before being stored in Cloud Firestore. Encryption keys are derived from the user's sign-in password.

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are used to provide authentication and cloud storage services. Data is transmitted using secure HTTPS connections.

Firebase also applies its own security measures, including encryption in transit and at rest.

3. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate users.
  • To store and synchronize saved credentials.
  • To provide account management functionality.
  • To maintain application security and reliability.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information is processed through Firebase services to operate the application. We do not share saved credentials with third parties for advertising.

5. Account and Data Deletion

Users can delete their accounts directly from the app through the Delete Account option.
